A school designed for the full learning journey.

Strandford Institute of Learning supports learners from their first classroom experiences in Grade RR through the confidence and independence needed in Grade 12.

The crest inspires the character of the school: innovation, growth, knowledge, and careful expression. Those ideas guide every classroom, activity, and learner relationship.

Knowledge

Strong foundations, clear teaching, and purposeful academic progress.

Innovation

Modern thinking, problem solving, and confident use of technology.

Character
