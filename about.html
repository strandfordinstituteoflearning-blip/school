About Strandford

A complete school journey with one steady promise: expect success.

Strandford Institute of Learning supports children from Grade RR through Grade 12 with clear teaching, strong values, and a caring school culture.

Our Identity

Built around growth, knowledge, and character.

Strandford's promise is to nurture every learner, celebrate every ability, and grow confident futures from Grade RR through Grade 12.

Our motto, Expect Success, is more than a phrase. It reminds learners that steady effort, respectful conduct, and good support can open doors.

The brochure describes Strandford as a KwaZulu-Natal school specialising in matric for learners with additional learning needs, alongside individualised plans and support through the learning journey.

Purpose

To help every learner build the academic habits and personal confidence needed to move forward.

Values

Respect, responsibility, perseverance, honesty, and pride in meaningful work.

Community

A school-family partnership where parents, teachers, and learners work in the same direction.
